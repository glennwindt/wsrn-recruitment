Policy & Procedures
Updated August 2025
Code of Conduct

All members are expected to uphold respectful behavior, maintain professionalism, and adhere to community guidelines at all times.

Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is mandatory. Absences must be communicated in advance and may require documentation depending on the situation.

Safety Protocols

Emergency procedures, equipment usage, and health guidelines must be followed to ensure a safe environment for all participants.

Communication Standards

All official communication should be clear, timely, and respectful. Use designated channels for announcements and feedback.

Privacy & Data

Personal data is handled with care and in accordance with GDPR. Unauthorized sharing of information is strictly prohibited.

Review & Updates

Policies are reviewed quarterly. Feedback is welcome and can be submitted through the official suggestion form.

Accessibility Commitment

We strive to ensure all materials and environments are inclusive and accessible to individuals with diverse needs and abilities.

Conflict Resolution

Disputes are handled through a structured mediation process, prioritizing fairness, transparency, and mutual respect.
